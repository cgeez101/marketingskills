## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A mobile espresso and cocktail bar that rolls into your event, serves your guests, and leaves the space clean.
**What it does:** We bring a styled cart, a licensed and insured bartender or barista, and everything needed to serve espresso drinks by day and cocktails, mocktails, beer and wine by night. Clients pick a package, a menu, and a time window. We set up, serve, and break down.
**Product category:** Mobile bar service / mobile coffee cart / event bartending
**Product type:** Local event service
**Business model:** Per-event packages priced by guest count and hours, plus add-ons. [confirm prices]

## Target Audience
**Primary segments:**
1. **Couples planning weddings** (largest ticket; book 6 to 12 months out)
2. **Corporate & office events** (team days, conferences, open houses, client appreciation; repeat bookings)
3. **Private parties** (birthdays, showers, graduations, holiday parties)
4. **Event partners** who refer us: wedding planners, venues, photographers, realtors, florists

**Jobs to be done:**
- "Give my guests something they'll talk about without me managing it."
- "Make our office event feel like more than pizza in the break room."
- "Handle the drinks so I don't have to hire, stock, and supervise a bar."

## Problems & Pain Points
- Venue bar packages are expensive and generic, or the venue has no bar at all
- DIY drinks means someone (often the host) ends up working the party
- Catering coffee is a lukewarm urn in the corner
- Worry about liability, permits, and over-serving

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other mobile bar and coffee cart companies. Falls short when: slow replies, vague pricing, cookie-cutter menus.
**Secondary:** Venue in-house bar, caterer's drink service. Falls short: high minimums, no personality.
**Indirect:** DIY self-serve bar, Starbucks traveler boxes. Falls short: host does the work, guests get a worse drink.

## Differentiation
- **Coffee and cocktails from one cart, one booking.** Espresso bar for the ceremony or morning session, cocktail bar for the reception or happy hour. [confirm you offer both]
- **Custom signature drinks** named after the couple, company, or guest of honor
- **Licensed, insured, and TABC/RBS/local-equivalent certified staff** [confirm certification name for your state]
- **Fast, clear quotes** (reply within [24] hours with a real number)

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "It's cheaper to buy the alcohol myself." | You still buy the alcohol on our dry-hire package; we bring the bar, mixers, ice, garnish, cups, and a certified bartender, so your liability and your evening are covered. |
| "Will you fit our venue?" | The cart needs about [6x8 ft] and a standard outlet. We can run off a generator if needed. Send us the venue name and we'll check. |
| "Is it worth it for a small event?" | Packages start at [25] guests and [2] hours. Small events are where a cart stands out most. |

**Anti-persona:** Events wanting a cheap, unlimited open bar with no limits on service, or very large (500+) events needing multiple full bars. [confirm]
