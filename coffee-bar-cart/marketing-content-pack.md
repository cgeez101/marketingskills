## 2. The Offer (from the `offers` skill)

The offer matters more than the copy. Three packages make it easy to say yes to the middle one.

| Package | What's included | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| **The Espresso Bar** | 2 hrs barista service, espresso drinks + 2 seasonal lattes, oat/whole milk, cups, setup + cleanup. Up to 50 guests. | $[550] |
| **The Signature Bar** *(most booked)* | 4 hrs, certified bartender, 2 custom signature cocktails + 1 mocktail, mixers, ice, garnish, cups, custom menu sign. Up to 100 guests. You supply alcohol (we send the shopping list). | $[1,100] |
| **Day-to-Night** | Espresso bar for the ceremony/morning + cocktail bar for the reception/evening. 6 hrs, 2 staff, everything in Signature. Up to 150 guests. | $[1,900] |

**Add-ons:** extra hour $[150] · extra signature drink $[75] · espresso martini station $[200] · branded cup sleeves or drink stickers $[2/guest] · hot cocoa bar (winter) $[150] · champagne toast service $[100]

**Bonus stack for launch season (first 15 bookings):**
- Free custom-named signature drink (normally $[75])
- Free printed menu sign in your colors
- Free mocktail version of every cocktail, so non-drinkers get the same experience

**Guarantee:** "If we're late to set up, your first hour is free." Simple, specific, and it answers the biggest fear.

**Honest urgency:** We can only work [one or two] events per day, so dates fill. Use the real number of open Saturdays in each month you promote.

## 3. Website Homepage Copy (from the `copywriting` skill)

**Hero headline:** Espresso by day. Cocktails by night. One mobile bar for your whole event.

**Subheadline:** We bring the cart, the certified bartender, and the drinks menu. You get a bar your guests remember and an evening you actually get to enjoy. Serving [City] and [surrounding area].

**Primary CTA:** Check my date
**Secondary CTA:** See packages

**Trust strip:** Licensed & insured · [State] alcohol-service certified · Setup and cleanup included · Quotes within 24 hours

**How it works**
1. **Tell us about your event.** Date, guest count, venue. Takes a minute.
2. **Get your quote and drink menu.** A real price within 24 hours, plus signature drink ideas.
3. **We show up and pour.** We arrive [60] minutes early, set up, serve, and pack out.

**Who we serve**
- **Weddings:** A latte bar during cocktail hour or a signature drink named after the two of you. Guests line up for it and it ends up in your photos.
- **Corporate events:** Turn the all-hands, open house, or client day into something people show up early for. Branded cups and invoices that make finance happy.
- **Private parties:** Birthdays, showers, graduations, and holiday parties. You host. We handle every drink.

**FAQ**
- **Do you provide the alcohol?** In most packages you buy it (it's cheaper for you, and it's how [State] licensing works for mobile bars). We send an exact shopping list based on your guest count. [confirm local law]
- **How much space do you need?** About [6x8 feet] and a standard outlet. No outlet? We bring a quiet generator.
- **Do you serve non-drinkers?** Every cocktail menu includes a mocktail, and the espresso bar is alcohol-free.
- **How far do you travel?** [30] miles from [City] is included. Beyond that, $[1]/mile.
- **How do I hold my date?** A [30]% deposit and a signed agreement.

**Closing CTA section:** Saturdays in [peak months] go first. Send your date and we'll tell you today if it's open. **[Check my date]**

**Meta title:** Mobile Coffee & Cocktail Bar in [City] | [Business Name]
**Meta description:** Mobile espresso cart and cocktail bar for weddings, corporate events, and parties in [City]. Certified bartenders, setup included. Get a quote in 24 hours.

## 5. Instagram & TikTok Content (from the `social` skill)

### Content pillars
| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| The drinks | 35% | Pour shots, latte art, cocktail builds, menu reveals |
| Events we work | 25% | Setups, guest reactions, venue shots (with permission) |
| Behind the cart | 20% | Building the cart, prep days, early mornings, mistakes |
| Helpful planning tips | 15% | "How much alcohol for 100 guests," timing tips |
| Offers & booking | 5% | Open dates, packages, launch bonus |

### 10 ready-to-post captions

**1. Launch announcement (carousel: cart photos)**
> After [eight] months of building, sanding, and testing way too many espresso shots, the cart is done. [Business Name] is a mobile espresso and cocktail bar for weddings, office events, and parties around [City]. Swipe to see it from every angle. We're booking [Season] now, and the first 15 events get a free signature drink named after them. Link in bio to check your date.

**2. Origin story (talking-head Reel)**
> I spent [X years] behind a bar at [type of place] and kept noticing the same thing at every wedding: the line at the bar was where people actually talked to each other. So I built a bar that can go anywhere. Here's how the cart came together.

**3. Signature drink idea**
> Every couple gets a drink named after them. Last weekend it was "The Jordan Mule" for Jordan and Sam: ginger beer, lime, and a splash of the bourbon from their first date. What would yours be called? Tell us your names and we'll suggest one in the replies.

**4. Planning tip (carousel)**
> How much alcohol do you need for 100 guests over 4 hours? Rough rule: one drink per guest per hour. That's about 400 drinks. Swipe for how we split that between beer, wine, and signature cocktails, and save this for your planning doc.

**5. Corporate pitch**
> Your next team meeting could have a barista. We set up in your lobby or break room at 8am, make lattes and cold brew for two hours, and leave before the first lunch order. Teams of 20 to 200. DM "OFFICE" for a quote.

**6. Day-to-Night**
> Ceremony at 3, reception at 6. We start with iced lattes while guests find their seats, then flip the cart to cocktails before the first toast. Same cart, same crew, no extra vendor to coordinate.

**7. Behind the cart**
> 5:40am on an event day: grind beans, pack 40 lbs of ice, prep three kinds of syrup, check the generator twice. Guests see the 4 hours we pour. This is the other 3.

**8. Mocktail spotlight**
> Not everyone drinks, and those guests deserve better than a can of soda. Every menu we build has a mocktail that gets the same glass, garnish, and attention. This one: [blackberry, lime, rosemary, soda].

**9. Seasonal menu**
> Fall menu is live: [Maple Pecan Latte], [Apple Cider Old Fashioned], and a [Spiced Pear] mocktail. Available for any event through December.

**10. Open dates**
> Three Saturdays left in [Month]: [dates]. If your event is on one of them, send us your guest count and venue and you'll have a quote today.

### 3 short video scripts (15 to 30 seconds)

**Reel A: "Watch the cart transform"**
- 0–2s: Text on screen: "Wedding at 3pm. Watch the cart."
- 2–10s: Time-lapse of espresso setup, guests grabbing iced lattes
- 10–20s: Quick cuts of swapping to cocktail menu sign, glassware, shaker
- 20–28s: Guests at the bar at golden hour
- End text: "Espresso → cocktails. One cart. Booking [Season]."
- Audio: trending upbeat sound

**Reel B: "Naming your signature drink" (talking head)**
- Hook (0–3s): "Give me your names and I'll make your wedding drink."
- Read a DM on screen, build the drink on camera in 15s
- Close: "Comment your names and I'll do yours next." (Repeat as a series; each reply is a new video.)

**Reel C: "POV: You hired a mobile bar"**
- POV text overlays: "You didn't buy ice." / "You didn't make a single drink." / "Your aunt asked who the bartender was." / "You danced the whole time."
- End: logo and "Link in bio to check your date."

### Posting schedule (first month)
- **Instagram:** 3 feed posts or Reels per week + daily Stories (prep, polls, open dates)
- **TikTok:** 3 to 5 videos per week; repost Reels
- **Facebook:** Post in local wedding, mom, and small-business groups 1 to 2x per week (follow each group's promo rules)
- **Pinterest:** Pin every setup and menu photo; brides plan there

**Preview:** Here's what happens next for your [event type].

> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks for asking about [Business Name] for your [event type] on [date]. I'm checking the calendar now and you'll have a quote and a few drink ideas within 24 hours.
>
> While you wait, two questions that help me build your menu: Is there a drink that means something to you (first date, a trip, a family recipe)? And will you have many non-drinkers?
>
> Just reply to this email.
>
> [Your name]
> [Business Name] · [phone]

### Quote follow-up sequence (if they haven't booked)

**Email 1, day 2: The quote**
**Subject:** Your bar for [date]: quote + menu ideas
> Hi [First name], here's your quote for [guest count] guests at [venue]: **$[amount]** for the [Package] package. That covers [hours], [staff], setup, cleanup, and two signature drinks. Ideas for yours: [Drink 1] and [Drink 2]. To hold your date, it's a [30]% deposit here: [link].

**Email 2, day 5: Answer the likely objection**
**Subject:** Quick question about [venue]
> Hi [First name], I wanted to check whether [venue] has a bar policy or outside-vendor requirements. Some venues ask for our insurance certificate, which I can send directly to them. Want me to reach out to their coordinator?

**Email 3, day 10: Honest urgency, then close the loop**
**Subject:** Holding [date] for you
> Hi [First name], I've had another inquiry for [date] and wanted to give you first call. If you'd like it, the deposit link is here: [link]. If your plans changed, no problem. Just reply "pass" and I'll stop following up.

### Post-event email (day after)
**Subject:** Thank you, and one small favor
> Hi [First name], thank you for having us last night. [One specific detail: "Your grandmother ordering a second espresso martini made our night."] If you have a minute, a Google review helps other couples find us: [link]. And if you know anyone planning an event, they'll get [$50 off] when they mention your name, and you'll get [$50] too.

## 7. Partner Outreach (planners, venues, photographers)

Partners are the fastest path to bookings for event businesses. Send to 10 per week.

**Email or DM**
**Subject:** A bar option for venues without one
> Hi [Name], I run [Business Name], a mobile espresso and cocktail cart in [City]. I noticed [Venue/your recent event at X] and wondered how you handle drinks for clients when there's no in-house bar.
>
> We set up in a 6x8 space, carry our own insurance, and can send the certificate ahead of time. I'd love to bring coffee to your team one morning so you can see the setup. No pitch, just lattes. Would [day] or [day] work?
>
> [Your name] · [phone] · [Instagram]

**Partner perk:** 10% referral fee or a free espresso bar for their own team event after every 3 referrals.

## 9. 30-Day Launch Plan

|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get findable | Claim Google Business Profile, set up Instagram/TikTok bio, publish homepage, list on The Knot / WeddingWire / Thumbtack / Yelp |
